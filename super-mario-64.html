Super Mario 64 loads straight in the browser, so there is no waiting on downloads, no account wall, and no setup before the action starts.

Why players keep coming back

Super Mario 64 throws you into a shared space where growth and territory come at the expense of everyone else in the round. The risk of losing everything in one bad decision is what keeps matches tense from the opening seconds.

A quick tip

Watching what causes a run to end in Super Mario 64 teaches you more than watching what lets it continue. Most improvement comes from spotting the one habit that keeps costing you a life or a match.
