Why players keep coming back

Drive Mad is about reading the road a split second before you reach it. Ramps, drops, and tight turns punish careless driving, so the fun comes from figuring out exactly how much throttle and angle a section actually needs.

A quick tip

Watching what causes a run to end in Drive Mad teaches you more than watching what lets it continue. Most improvement comes from spotting the one habit that keeps costing you a life or a match.
