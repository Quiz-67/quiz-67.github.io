About Stack

Stack is one of those browser games you can jump into the moment a page finishes loading, with nothing to install and nothing standing between you and the first round.

Why players keep coming back

Stack rewards patience over speed. Each level is a small logic problem, and the satisfaction comes from spotting the trick rather than mashing buttons faster. It's a good pick when you want a game that makes you think instead of react.

A quick tip

New players usually improve fastest by playing a handful of short rounds in a row instead of one long session — Stack rewards pattern recognition, and that only builds up with repetition.

FAQ

Is Stack free to play?
Yes, Stack runs directly in the browser on Quiz 67 at no cost and does not require an account to start playing.

Do I need to download anything to play Stack?
No download is required. Stack loads inside the page and runs using your browser, the same way any other embedded game on this site does.

Can I play Stack on a school or work computer?
Stack is a lightweight browser game, so it typically runs fine on Chromebooks and standard office or school browsers, though local network rules can vary.
