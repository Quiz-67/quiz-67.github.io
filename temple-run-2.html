About Temple Run 2

Temple Run 2 is one of those browser games you can jump into the moment a page finishes loading, with nothing to install and nothing standing between you and the first round.

Why players keep coming back

Temple Run 2 leans on old-school arcade pacing: quick to understand, hard to master, and always tempting you into one more attempt after a run ends. Score chasing does a lot of the work here.

A quick tip

Watching what causes a run to end in Temple Run 2 teaches you more than watching what lets it continue. Most improvement comes from spotting the one habit that keeps costing you a life or a match.
