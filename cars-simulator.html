About Cars Simulator

Cars Simulator is built for quick access. There's no client to fetch, no login screen, just a game that starts when you click it.

Why players keep coming back

Cars Simulator is about reading the road a split second before you reach it. Ramps, drops, and tight turns punish careless driving, so the fun comes from figuring out exactly how much throttle and angle a section actually needs.

A quick tip

Cars Simulator doesn't punish slow starts as much as it punishes rushed decisions, so it's worth taking the first attempt at a normal pace before pushing for speed.
