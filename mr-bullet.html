About Mr Bullet

Mr Bullet is built for quick access. There's no client to fetch, no login screen, just a game that starts when you click it.

Why players keep coming back

Mr Bullet is built around aim and positioning rather than pure reaction speed. Waves get busier as you go, and staying alive means reading where pressure is coming from before it's too late to reposition.

A quick tip

If an early attempt at Mr Bullet goes badly, restarting immediately is almost always better than trying to grind out a bad run. Short resets keep the pace of the game working in your favor.

FAQ

Is Mr Bullet free to play?
Yes, Mr Bullet runs directly in the browser on Quiz 67 at no cost and does not require an account to start playing.

Do I need to download anything to play Mr Bullet?
No download is required. Mr Bullet loads inside the page and runs using your browser, the same way any other embedded game on this site does.

Can I play Mr Bullet on a school or work computer?
Mr Bullet is a lightweight browser game, so it typically runs fine on Chromebooks and standard office or school browsers, though local network rules can vary.
