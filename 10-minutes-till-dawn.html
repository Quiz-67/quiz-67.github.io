About 10 Minutes Till Dawn

10 Minutes Till Dawn is one of those browser games you can jump into the moment a page finishes loading, with nothing to install and nothing standing between you and the first round.

Why players keep coming back

10 Minutes Till Dawn keeps its mechanics simple on purpose. The learning curve is short, but there's enough depth in later stages that improving still feels earned rather than automatic.

A quick tip

Watching what causes a run to end in 10 Minutes Till Dawn teaches you more than watching what lets it continue. Most improvement comes from spotting the one habit that keeps costing you a life or a match.
