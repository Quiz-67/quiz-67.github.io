About Rooftop Snipers

Rooftop Snipers keeps things direct: open the tab, hit play, and the game is already teaching you the controls through the first few seconds.

Why players keep coming back

Rooftop Snipers is built around aim and positioning rather than pure reaction speed. Waves get busier as you go, and staying alive means reading where pressure is coming from before it's too late to reposition.

A quick tip

New players usually improve fastest by playing a handful of short rounds in a row instead of one long session — Rooftop Snipers rewards pattern recognition, and that only builds up with repetition.
