Why players keep coming back

1v1.LOL earns its replay value from other people. Facing a live opponent changes the rhythm of every match — patterns that work against a script fall apart against a person reacting in real time, which is exactly why players keep queuing up for one more round.

A quick tip

Watching what causes a run to end in 1v1.LOL teaches you more than watching what lets it continue. Most improvement comes from spotting the one habit that keeps costing you a life or a match.
