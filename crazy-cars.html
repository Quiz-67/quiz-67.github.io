About Crazy Cars

Crazy Cars keeps things direct: open the tab, hit play, and the game is already teaching you the controls through the first few seconds.

Why players keep coming back

Crazy Cars is about reading the road a split second before you reach it. Ramps, drops, and tight turns punish careless driving, so the fun comes from figuring out exactly how much throttle and angle a section actually needs.

A quick tip

Crazy Cars doesn't punish slow starts as much as it punishes rushed decisions, so it's worth taking the first attempt at a normal pace before pushing for speed.
